Activity 1

Fruitful Design

Fruit Drawing Using Elements & Principles of Design

This activity focuses on illustrating a fruit using the elements and principles of design. I choose an avocado as my subject to explore how basic visual elements can be combined to create a balanced, detailed, and visually appealing drawing.

DESCRIPTION

For this activity, I created a hand-drawn illustration of an avocado, applying different elements and principles of design to represent its natural appearance. I explored the use of lines, shapes, forms, colors, and textures to capture the avocado's unique characteristics. Through this drawing, I practiced observing details, maintaning proper proportions, and creating visual balance. This activity helped me understand how the elements and principles of design contribute to the overall appearance and composition of an artwork.

THE ORIGINAL SKETCH

AVOCADO

This is my hand-drawn illustration of an avocado with the application of the elements and principles of design.

Element and Principle Application

  • Line - Defines the avocado's outline and details.
  • Shape - Shows its recognizable oval or pear-like form.
  • Color - Represents the natural green and brown tones.
  • Texture - Adds visual detail to the skin and inner flesh.
  • Form - Creates the appearance of a three-dimensional fruit.
